Demo scenario

Compare oversampling, then evaluate on test data

  1. Generate samples Oversample with k = 5, p = 3, seed = 2207; generate m = n⁻ − n⁺ synthetic points.
  2. Train Standardize from training data only, then fit KNeighborsClassifier(n_neighbors=5).
  3. Results Single fixed holdout: recall, precision, F1, MCC, FN, FP, plus the decision boundary.
  4. Compare Imbalanced, Gaussian, Random, Global, SMOTE, and Simplicial SMOTE.

n⁺ = minority · n⁻ = majority · m = n⁻ − n⁺ full balance target.
