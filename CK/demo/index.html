Simplicial SMOTE

SMOTE generates points along k-NN edges; Simplicial SMOTE generates points inside a simplex using barycentric coordinates. Choose a dataset below to enter the demo.

Demo scenario

Compare oversampling, then evaluate on test data

  1. Generate samples Oversample the minority class with k = 5, p = 3, and seed = 2207; generate m = n⁻ − n⁺ points.
  2. Train Train LogisticRegression(C=1.2, class_weight=None, max_iter=2000, random_state=seed) on the balanced training set.
  3. Results Evaluate on the test set: recall, precision, F1, FN, FP (and the boundary on the canvas).
  4. Compare Three methods: Original (no oversampling), SMOTE, and Simplicial SMOTE.

n⁺ = minority (positive, the class to augment) · n⁻ = majority (negative, the target count) · m = n⁻ − n⁺ to balance the two classes.
